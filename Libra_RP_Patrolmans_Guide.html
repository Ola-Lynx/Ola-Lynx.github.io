General sentencing

  • Maximum imprisonment period: 50 months.
  • Class-E: 10 months + $1,000.
  • Class-D: 20 months + $2,000.
  • Class-C: 30 months + $5,000.
  • Class-B: 40 months + $8,000.
  • Class-A: 50 months + $10,000; no bail.

Misdemeanors

  • 1 point: $1,000, no imprisonment.
  • 2 points: $2,000, no imprisonment.
  • 3 points: $5,000, no imprisonment.
  • 4 points: $8,000, no imprisonment.
  • 5 points: $10,000, no imprisonment.
  • More than 5 combined points may permit up to 20 months + up to $25,000 under §1.9.2.

Prohibited items (§2.2)

  • Any firearms with an unidentifiable serial number
  • State-issued weapons or melees
  • Ballistic vests with state markings
  • Self-manufactured ballistic vests (red, green, purple, blue, yellow)
  • Lockpicks
  • Counterfeit money
  • Vault Hand Drills
  • Unauthorized motor vehicle license plates and paint

Important principles

  • Presumption of innocence.
  • Crime must be supported by evidence before conviction.
  • Performance of duty can exempt criminal liability when lawful, reasonable, and within the Code of Procedural Law.
  • Self-defense and defense of others are addressed in §1.4.
